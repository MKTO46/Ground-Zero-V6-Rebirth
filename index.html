DAILY OUTDOOR MISSION · THE TERRITORY

FULL CIRCUIT. EVERY DAY.

Mon–Thu: your established North Mount Carmel route, starting/finishing by the Field 2 gate; walk the connected outer perimeter and jog your existing sections by Field 14, Fields 5–4, Fields 3–1, and toward Field 2. Fri–Sun: cover the same distance on a treadmill or another route when soccer games block access.

Do not shorten or replace your circuit with a timer workout. Use your GPS running app to measure the distance once; that becomes your treadmill/backup mileage. Each 14-day review, consider adding 10–15 seconds to one or more jogging sections only if comfortable — never automatically.

Warm-ups are preparation sets, not working sets. All programmed strength movements default to THREE working sets. Reduce or stop a painful movement rather than forcing extra sets.
